Our Story

Born from a love of genuine craft

Authentic Coffee started with one small roaster and a belief that great coffee deserves to be understood — not just consumed. Today we roast every batch in-house and build direct relationships with the farmers who grow our beans.

Direct trade sourcing

We visit every farm we work with, ensuring fair pay and sustainable practices at the source.

Small-batch roasting

Every roast is done in batches under 15kg, giving us precise control over flavour development.

Community-rooted

We partner with local schools, host monthly cuppings, and keep our community at the centre.
